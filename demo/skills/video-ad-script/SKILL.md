# Video ad script

1. One line per shot: what we see, then voiceover or on-screen text.
2. Shot 1 is the hook (under 2 s): a visual surprise or a question.
3. Middle shots show one benefit each. No more than one claim per shot.
4. Last shot: product, name, call to action.
5. Keep the shot count from the requirements.
